# Scene Guide

## 00_Foundation

This scene establishes the shared platformer foundation used by all later Enemy AI demonstrations.

- Move with **A/D** or the **Left/Right Arrow** keys.
- Jump with **Space**.
- The camera follows the player horizontally within the arena bounds.
- The ground and raised blocks use the `Ground` physics layer.
- No Enemy AI is included in this milestone.

The scene is generated by `FoundationSceneBuilder` so its required references can be recreated consistently from the Unity menu:

`HUST Enemy AI Lab > Build Milestone 1 Foundation`

Running the builder replaces the generated foundation scene and shared foundation prefabs.

## 01_Prototype

This scene demonstrates the smallest reasonable implementation of the first Enemy AI requirement.

- The same `Player` and `DemoArena` prefabs from Milestone 1 are reused.
- One Slime patrols horizontally between two visible yellow patrol markers.
- Patrol speed is configurable on the `PrototypeSlime` component.
- The Slime reverses and flips its visual at each boundary.
- Press **R** to reload the scene for a clean demonstration.
- There is no State Machine, `EnemyBrain`, behavior interface, or generic Enemy framework.

The scene and prototype prefab can be regenerated from the Unity menu:

`HUST Enemy AI Lab > Build Milestone 2 Prototype`

Running the Milestone 2 builder replaces only `01_Prototype`, its prototype Slime prefab, and its prototype visual assets. It does not rebuild `00_Foundation`.

## 02_EnumFSM

This scene shows the next step in the Slime's growth: one `FsmSlime` class with an explicit `EnemyState` enum.

- **Patrol (white):** move between the two patrol points and reverse at each boundary.
- **Chase (yellow):** enter when the Player is within detection range and move horizontally toward them.
- **Attack (red):** enter within attack range, stop, wind up, apply one validated impact, then observe a cooldown.
- Attack returns to Chase when the Player leaves attack range. Chase and Attack return to Patrol when the Player leaves detection range.
- Leaving attack range during wind-up cancels the attack, so no stale target damage is applied.
- Move with **A/D** or **Arrow Keys**, jump with **Space**, and press **R** to reset.

Main files:

- `Scripts/Runtime/Intermediate/FsmSlime.cs`
- `Prefabs/Enemies/Intermediate/FsmSlime.prefab`
- `Scenes/02_EnumFSM.unity`
- `Tests/PlayMode/FsmSlimeTests.cs`

The scene and FSM prefab can be generated from:

`HUST Enemy AI Lab > Build Milestone 3 Enum FSM`

The builder reuses the accepted Player, DemoArena, Slime sprite, and patrol marker assets. It creates only the separate Milestone 3 scene and FSM prefab, and appends the scene after 00 and 01 in Build Settings.

## 03_ModularAI

Scene 03 preserves the rules from Scene 02 but distributes them across focused components:

- `EnemyBrain` owns `EnemyState`, evaluates every transition, and coordinates the other components. It neither queries physics nor applies damage.
- `EnemySensor` exclusively performs Player-layer detection and exposes safe target and range information.
- `EnemyMovement` owns patrol, chase velocity, stopping, direction, and sprite flipping.
- `EnemyCombat` owns wind-up, impact, cooldown, cancellation, impact-time validation, and duplicate-impact prevention.
- `EnemyHealth` owns runtime HP, one-time death, white hit feedback, and darkened death feedback.
- `EnemyConfig` stores reusable tuning only; HP state, targets, and timers remain on instances.
- `PlayerCombat` is intentionally minimal: press **F** near the Slime to deal one damage.

`EnemyBrain` asks the sensor for facts, explicitly selects the next enum state, then coordinates movement or combat. Compared with `FsmSlime`, this makes physics queries, motion, attack timing, and health independently testable. The tradeoff is more components, references, setup work, and coordination rules.

| Scene | Structure | Behaviors | Teaching focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01_Prototype | One patrol MonoBehaviour | Patrol | Smallest reasonable solution |
| 02_EnumFSM | One enum-FSM MonoBehaviour | Patrol, Chase, Attack | Growing responsibilities |
| 03_ModularAI | Brain plus focused components and config | Equivalent Patrol, Chase, Attack | Selective separation for maintenance and testing |

Controls: **A/D** or arrows to move, **Space** to jump, **F** to attack, and **R** to reset. State colors are green, yellow, and red; hits flash white and death darkens the Slime.

Generate the scene with `HUST Enemy AI Lab > Build Milestone 4 Modular AI`.
