# Milestone 1 Scene Guide

## 00_Foundation

This scene establishes the shared platformer foundation used by all later Enemy AI demonstrations.

- Move with **A/D** or the **Left/Right Arrow** keys.
- Jump with **Space**.
- The camera follows the player horizontally within the arena bounds.
- The ground and raised blocks use the `Ground` physics layer.
- No Enemy AI is included in this milestone.

The scene is generated by `FoundationSceneBuilder` so its required references can be recreated consistently from the Unity menu:

`HUST Enemy AI Lab > Build Milestone 1 Foundation`

Running the builder replaces the generated foundation scene and shared foundation prefabs.
